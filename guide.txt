2. UPDATED PROJECT PROPOSAL
===========================

Computer Graphics and Image Processing Laboratory
Project Proposal
Roll No. 2107064
Project Title: Village Gathering by the River – A Bangladeshi Rural Night Scene in 3D
1. Project Description
The project recreates a typical Bangladeshi village on a summer night, when neighbours gather in a courtyard beside a flowing river. On a woven charpai, elders chat and fan themselves with handmade wooden fans; children play nearby, and siblings read a Bengali poem aloud. On the river, a boatman rows a traditional wooden boat, while hens and ducks wander the yard. The full moon and fireflies form a supporting nighttime atmosphere, not the main focus.
Every object is generated procedurally in OpenGL 3.3 core-profile code from mathematical primitives (spheres, cylinders, cones, planes) — no external models. A single directional “moon” light drives per-pixel Phong shading (ambient + diffuse + specular), while hierarchical and time-varying transformations animate the fan, boatman, river, children, animals and trees. Animation follows clear stages: settling in → river and boat movement → village gathering → poem reading → firefly glow.
2. Objectives
•	Communicate traditional rural Bangladesh through a river, boat, houses, trees, animals and a gathering.
•	Apply the OpenGL 3.3 pipeline (VAO/VBO/EBO, GLSL) with procedural geometry.
•	Demonstrate hierarchical modeling and time-varying transformations.
•	Use moonlit Phong lighting to support the village scene.
3. Objects Used
•	River: subdivided grid with sine-wave vertex displacement for flowing water; sloped banks.
•	Boat and boatman: hull from scaled cylinder/sphere segments; shoulder → arm → oar hierarchy rows as the boat glides and bobs.
•	Village houses (1–2): mud-walled cuboids, thatched cone/prism roofs, cylinder verandah posts.
•	Terrain: parametric plane grid, tinted dark grey-green.
•	Charpai and fan: cylinder legs, criss-cross cuboid weave; hub with rotating rectangular blades.
•	Villagers: procedural humanoid rigs (spheres, cylinders); flattened cuboid books.
•	Trees and plants: cylinder trunks, sphere/cone canopies (palm, banana, bamboo).
•	Hens and ducks: sphere/cone bodies, heads and beaks; waddling and pecking motion.
•	Fireflies and moon: small emissive spheres with sine-based blink; large distant sphere as light source.
4. Extra Features (Beyond Baseline)
•	Multi-part rigged characters (shoulder → arm, hip → leg).
•	Emissive firefly glow and moon-reflection specular on the river, without bloom.
•	Instanced-style rendering: one shared VBO/EBO reused for fireflies, leaves and animals.
•	Stage-based scene director with fullscreen alpha cross-fades.
•	Wind-sway animation, seeded pseudo-random population, and orbital camera fly-through.
5. Expected Outcome
An animated 3D scene conveying Bangladeshi village life beside a river, demonstrating lighting, hierarchical modeling, procedural geometry and staged animation, with moonlight enhancing the mood.
